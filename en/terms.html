Service terms

General terms for estimates, mobile work-space conditions, parts, authorization and scheduling.

Estimates

An initial estimate may change after inspection if the vehicle condition, required parts or job complexity differs from the information provided.

Authorization

Additional work should not be performed without explaining it and receiving approval from the customer.

Safe work area

The customer must provide a legal, stable and reasonably safe work location with enough space. Work may be postponed or declined because of weather, traffic, unstable ground or other safety concerns.

Parts

Parts, brands and warranties are confirmed for each job. Customer-supplied parts may have different labor or warranty conditions.

Scheduling and cancellation

Appointments are confirmed through WhatsApp. Cancellation conditions, reserved time and any travel charge are communicated before scheduling.

Hours and availability

There is currently no fixed public schedule. Appointments are confirmed through WhatsApp based on availability.

Payment methods

Cash and bank transfer are accepted. Any deposit or payment condition must be confirmed before service.

Primary area and travel

The primary operating area is Dubuque, Iowa. Farther locations may include an additional travel charge that is disclosed and accepted before scheduling.

Warranty

Any warranty must be stated in writing for the specific repair, including its duration, covered parts or labor and exclusions.

Jobs requiring a shop

Some repairs cannot be completed on-site and may require towing, a lift, specialized programming or shop-only equipment.
