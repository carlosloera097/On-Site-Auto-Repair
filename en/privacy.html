Privacy notice

How Tri State Drive Fix handles information submitted through the website and WhatsApp.

Information used by the site

The estimate form runs in the customer’s browser. Its fields are used to prepare a WhatsApp message; the website does not send that form to its own database.

WhatsApp

When the customer chooses to send the message, WhatsApp processes the information under its own terms and privacy practices.

Purpose

The information is used to answer questions, review service availability, prepare an estimate and follow up on a requested job.

Data minimization

Only send information needed to identify the vehicle, location and requested service. Do not send sensitive financial or identity documents through the form.

Deletion requests

Requests regarding messages held by the business may be sent through WhatsApp at +1 (608) 426-2849.
